# Synthetic Reading Note

This fictional exercise uses readings in points. It is not clinical guidance.

## §1 — Reading review

Review the synthetic reading when it is at least 0.1 points. Below 0.1 points
the instruction does not apply. If the reading is unknown, ask for it.

For this exercise, assign modeled support of 20% to review below 0.3 points,
50% at exactly 0.3 points, and 90% above 0.3 points. Assign the remaining
support to deferring review. When the instruction applies, these rows give
Do-Not-Consider Scores of 80%, 50%, and 10%, respectively. The score is not a
clinical probability.
